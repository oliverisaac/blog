- 6 servings rice: 350g water + 270g rice  
- 900 g raw chicken
- 600 g yellow potatoes, diced
- diced peppers and onions 
- 60 grams [mae ploy curry paste](https://www.amazon.com/Mae-Ploy-Yellow-Curry-Paste/dp/B002N60DA6?tag=isaacinit-20) 
- 1 can coconut milk (do not shake!)  
- 1 can chicken broth  
- brown sugar 1-2tbsp (to taste
- fish sauce (to taste)
- 170 g greek yogurt

Make the rice  in your rice cooker. 

Put the curry paste in your pot

Add the top quarter of the can of coconut milk as well.  

Cook the curry milk combination until the aroma releases from the curry paste. it should be bubbling and thicken up quite a bit. If it starts to stick to the bottom of the pan then you've gone too long (it's not ruined, but this is a clear sign you went too long.)

Add the rest of the coconut milk.

Add the peppers/onions.

Fill the can with chicken broth and put that in as well.  

> [!Tip]
> 
> You can use chicken bullion instead of chicken broth.  

Add the potatoes

Bring to a boil  

Boil for 10 minutes or until potatoes are close to done  

Simmer longer if you want less liquid.

Add the raw chicken.

Boil for another 3 minutes until chicken is done (165 internal temp, or 155 internal temp for at least 1 minute)

> Note that you can cook chicken to a lower temp as long as it stays at that temperature for the required time.
