- 6 servings chicken 
- 6 servings yellow potatoes, diced
- diced peppers and onions 
- 60 grams curry paste  
- 1 can coconut milk (do not shake!)  
- 1 can chicken broth  
- a bit of coconut oil  
- 6 servings rice: 405g water + 270g rice  
- brown sugar 1-2tbsp (to taste
- fish sauce (to taste)

Make the rice  
Put the coconut oil and curry paste in the pan.  
Add the top quarter of the coconut milk as well.  
Add the peppers/onions  
Stir until the paste carmalizes  
Add the rest of the milk.  
Fill the can with chicken broth and put that in as well.  
Add potatoes  
Bring to a boil  
Boil for 10 minutes or until potatoes are close to done  
Add chicken  
Boil for another 3 minutes until chicken is done
