Makes 6 servings

- 336 g uncooked pasta (elbow mac or shells)
- 600 g cottage cheese
- 150 g milk
- 1-1/2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1-1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 90 g cheddar cheese
- 180 g mozzarella cheese
- 600 g cooked chicken, diced

Make the pasta and drain in a colander. 

While the pasta is cooking, combine the cottage cheese, milk, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper. Blend.

Once the pasta is cooked, drain and set aside. 

In the pot the pasta was cooked in, add the cottage cheese mix as well as the chedder and mozzarella cheese. Heat on low until the cheese is melted. 

Once the cheese is melted, put back in your bowl for blending and blend until smooth again.

Combine the cooked chicken, the pasta, and the cheese sauce. 

Divide into 6 servings.
