1. Beat the raw chicken breasts iwth something heavy. [Use a meat tenderizer if you have one](https://amzn.to/3KdqM7N) 
2. Make a seasoning powder mix of your desired flavors.
    1. Shoot for salt to be 1.5% the weight of your chicken
        1. E.g. if you have 900g of chicken, use 13.5g of salt
    2. Good combinations include:
        1. garlic, pepper, and paprika
3. Line a baking tray with foil. Drizzle some olive oil into the tray.
4. Lay each chick breast onto the tray, ensuring each side is coated with olive oil.
5. Sprinkle your seasoning powder onto both sides of the chicken.
6. Cook at 400º until the inside reaches 165º F
        1. Keep in mind that you can cook it for less time as long as you reach the required time for [[./pasteurization_time_for_poultry.jpg|pasteurization of poultry]]
